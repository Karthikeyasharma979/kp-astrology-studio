The health aspect of the native is being examined from son’s horoscope in this 
article.  Son is born in the star of Rahu (Swathi) with ascendant Cancer on 29th 
November, 1986 in Hyderabad at 21.5.35 pm.  On rotation of the chart the lagna of 
the mother falls in Virgo sign at 28-43’5” degrees. 
K.P.Rule says that the native is prone to disease if the 6th cuspal sublord is a 
significator of lagna or vice versa.  If the sublord is connected to 8th house the disease 
will be chronic or incurable and if it is connected to 12th house it denotes 
hospitalization.  
In the rotated chart, Venus is the 6th cuspal sublord.  It is in the star of Rahu and sub 
of Saturn. Venus is a lord of 8th conjunct with lagna lord mercury, moon and posited 
in lagna.  The starlord of venus is rahu in 6th and occupies sign Pieces. The sublord 
Saturn conjunct with sun, 12th lord in maraca house 2.  So, venus signifies 1,6,8,12 
indicating severe chronic health problems. 
The lagna cuspal sublord is Saturn in the star and sub of mercury.  Saturn lord of 
4,5,6 conjunct with sun, 12th lord, is in 2 in the star and sub of mercury in 1, lord of 9.  
Mercury conjunct with venus, 8th lord, is aspected by Jupiter maraca and badhaka 
lord. So, the lagna sublord Saturn signifies 1,6,8,12 denoting incurable health 
problems. 
The lagna lord mercury, conjunct with venus 8th lord, is deposited in the star of 
Jupiter, badhaka and maraca lord and in the sub of Saturn, 6th lord (disease karaka) 
posited in 2 along with sun, 12th lord. Thus, the lagna lord mercury connected to 6 
(disease), 8 (chronic and incurable), 12 (requiring hospitalization), 2 (maraca), 7 
(badhaka and maraca). Kethu is also posited in lagna.
