BRIEF ON EPS(EXTENDED PLANETARY SIGNIFICATION SYSTEM) 
In K.P. planet and its star significations are considered. The significations are Cuspal lordship, 
Occupation. Subsequently sublord of the planet is also considered  with similar significations.. 
In EPS, Extended significations are considered…Along with CL (Cuspal Lord), OCC (Occupation) the 
significations additionally considered are CST (Cuspal starlord) and CSL (Cuspal sublord)...Aspect 
and Conjunction supports are also seen like this. 
Planets rulers are star lord (Level2), starlord of starlord (Level 2A), sublord (Level 3), and starlord of 
sublord (Level 4).
